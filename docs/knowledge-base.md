## General

### Overview
West Virginia State University (WVSU) is a public, 1890 land-grant, historically Black college/university (HBCU) in Institute, West Virginia. It is a masters-degree-granting institution described as the largest institution of higher education in the Charleston metropolitan area, and is known as a 'living laboratory of human relations'.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/about/administration/institutional-research/wvsu-quick-facts.aspx

### Founding and name changes
Founded in 1891 as the West Virginia Colored Institute and designated one of the original 1890 land-grant schools under the Second Morrill Act. Renamed West Virginia Collegiate Institute (1915), West Virginia State College (1929), and West Virginia State University (2004).

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/about/administration/institutional-research/wvsu-quick-facts.aspx

### Motto
Veritas Lux Mundi (Latin), meaning 'Truth is the Light of the World'.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_State_University

### Mission
WVSU will meet the higher education and economic development needs of the state and region through innovative teaching and applied research.

Source: https://issuu.com/wvsupublications/docs/2019_2020_viewbook_04012020

### Accreditation
Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/about/administration/institutional-research/wvsu-quick-facts.aspx

### Land-grant status
One of only 19 land-grant universities designated by the Second Morrill Act in 1890 and one of only two land-grant institutions in West Virginia. Land-grant extension services aid more than 20,000 West Virginians in 38 counties. Research funding about $17 million annually (per the 2019-2021 quick facts).

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/about/administration/institutional-research/wvsu-quick-facts.aspx

### Enrollment and student profile
Quick facts (older data): total enrollment Fall 2019 was 4,337; student/faculty ratio 12:1. Undergraduate profile 2020-21: 58% female, 89% in-state, 11% adult (25+), 14% minority, 63% part-time, 5% transfer, 5% international. Third-party sites in recent years report about 3,000 to 3,800 total students, so enrollment figures vary by source and year.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/about/administration/institutional-research/wvsu-quick-facts.aspx

### Outcomes
Fall-to-fall retention for full-time first-time freshmen was 60%. The 4-year graduation rate was 16% and the 6-year rate 31% (2015 cohort). 87% of alumni within six months of graduation are employed full-time, in full-time graduate school, or in the military (May 2020 cohort). 90% of students are eligible for financial assistance.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/about/administration/institutional-research/wvsu-quick-facts.aspx

### Campus and location
Located in Institute, WV 25112, about 8 miles west of Charleston, the state capital. The campus is about 123 acres with 864,287 square feet of floor space.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/about/administration/institutional-research/wvsu-quick-facts.aspx


## Admissions

### Undergraduate admissions office
Office of Undergraduate Recruitment & Admissions, 124 Ferrell Hall, PO Box 1000, Institute, WV 25112. Email admissions@wvstateu.edu. A copy of an unofficial transcript can be used to make an admissions decision. WVSU has rolling admissions and test scores (SAT/ACT) are optional. Third-party sites report an acceptance rate of about 96%.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/?p=32228

### International undergraduate admissions
Checklist: $20 application fee, application for admission, official transcripts, proof of English language proficiency, and a statement of purpose. Documents can be emailed to international@wvstateu.edu; phone (304) 204-4094. A third-party site lists international deadlines of about August 14 (fall) and October 1 (spring); confirm on wvstateu.edu.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/admissions/international-student-admissions/international-undergraduate-student-admissions/


## Costs

Estimated annual figures: tuition and fees $9,920 (in-state) and about $15,560 (out-of-state); books and supplies $1,330; housing and meals about $10,718 on campus, $10,850 off campus, or $6,510 living with parents; loan fees $71. Online undergraduate programs: tuition and fees $11,640 plus $1,750 online class fees. Out-of-state label is inferred from the page layout; verify on the official page.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/admissions/financial-aid/2026-2027-cost-of-attendance/

### 2026-2027 cost of attendance (graduate)
In-state graduate tuition and fees $9,990 per year; books and supplies $1,330; housing and meals $9,760 on campus or $10,850 off campus; loan fees $155; transportation $1,380. Online graduate: tuition and fees $9,990 plus $1,500 online class fees, estimated total $12,975.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/admissions/financial-aid/2026-2027-cost-of-attendance/

## Academics

### Colleges
WVSU is reorganizing its academic colleges from four to three, so some web pages are in transition. One of the three is the College of Business, Sciences, and Technology. The College of Professional Studies covers areas such as Education, Health and Human Performance, Nursing, Social Work and Criminal Justice.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/academics/academic-colleges/

### Programs and degrees
Quick facts (2019-20): 72 academic programs, including 23 baccalaureate programs, 7 master's programs and 7 fully online programs. Examples include Biology, Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics, Engineering (Civil and Chemical emphasis), Business and Economics (concentrations such as Accounting, Finance, Marketing, Management and Management Information Systems), English, Music (BA), Health Science, Sport Studies, Education and Interdisciplinary Studies. Graduate: Biotechnology, Computer Science, Media Studies, Master of Education in Instructional Leadership, and EdD in Leadership Studies.

Source: https://www.wvstateu.edu/academics/academic-degrees


## Library

### Drain-Jordan Library overview
The Drain-Jordan Library holds nearly 150,000 book volumes plus e-books, e-journals, government documents, and non-print and archival resources. Website: library.wvstateu.edu and wvstateu.edu/students/current-students/library. Address: Drain-Jordan Library, WVSU, PO Box 1002, Campus Box L17, Institute, WV 25112-1002. Main phone (304) 766-3116; reference desk (304) 766-3135.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/students/current-students/library/

### Services and help
Ask-A-Librarian by chat or email (email answered within 48 hours Monday to Friday during semesters; chat only during library hours), reference desk email refdesk@wvstateu.edu, and in-person help during library hours. Laptops can be borrowed for 3-hour periods. Daily Charleston Gazette newspapers are in the Periodicals section; the student newspaper is online at YJOnline. Interlibrary loan (ILL) is available. Distance learners can return materials by book drop or mail.

Source: https://library.wvstateu.edu/askalibrarian.html

### Databases and LibGuides
The library offers research databases (including EBSCOhost) listed at library.wvstateu.edu/DB-sets/DB-index.html; not all databases work off campus. Subject guides are at wvstateu.libguides.com (for example Nursing Resources, Government Documents, and an Introduction to Library Resources). Older pathfinders cover subjects like Biology and Criminal Justice.

Source: https://library.wvstateu.edu/DB-sets/DB-index.html

### Archives and Special Collections
Located on the ground floor (Room 2) of the Drain-Jordan Library, by appointment during library hours. Holdings cover university history, African Americans in West Virginia, African-American higher education, West Virginia and Appalachian history, including rare books, photographs, manuscripts, recordings and maps. Contact refdesk@wvstateu.edu or 304-766-3135.

Source: https://library.wvstateu.edu/archivp.html

### Federal depository
The Drain-Jordan Library has been a member of the Federal Depository Library Program (FDLP) since 1907, providing free public access to government information.

Source: https://wvstateu.libguides.com/c.php?g=776402

## Cybersecurity Innovation Center and Faculty

### Cybersecurity Innovation Center
The WVSU Cybersecurity Innovation Center (CIC) advances cybersecurity education, applied research, and real-world innovation through hands-on labs, collaborative projects, and student-centered learning. It emphasizes critical-infrastructure protection, including agriculture and other essential sectors, and connects education, workforce development, research, community engagement, and industry.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/college-of-business-sciences-and-technology/cic/

### Dr. Ali Al-Sinayyid
Dr. Ali Al-Sinayyid is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Director of the WVSU Cybersecurity Innovation Center. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Southern Illinois University Carbondale and has more than 17 years of combined academic and industry experience. His work includes critical-infrastructure cybersecurity, operational technology and industrial control systems (OT/ICS/SCADA), CyberAI, Internet of Things security, cybersecurity education, workforce development, and applied research.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/college-of-business-sciences-and-technology/author/ali-alsinayyid/

### Cybersecurity degree and Google Cybersecurity Clinic
WVSU's four-year Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity was approved in 2025 and offers General Cybersecurity and Agricultural Cybersecurity options. The faculty profile identifies WVSU as the only university in West Virginia offering the Agricultural Cybersecurity option. Dr. Al-Sinayyid led development of the degree and directs the WVSU Google Cybersecurity Clinic. The profile lists a $1 million Google.org-supported clinic grant; the clinic opened in March 2025 as the first cybersecurity clinic of its type in West Virginia and provides no-cost cybersecurity support while giving students applied experience.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/college-of-business-sciences-and-technology/author/ali-alsinayyid/

### CIC hands-on labs
- CyberHive provides physical SCADA, PLC, and HMI environments for training in monitoring, attacking, and defending industrial systems such as chemical and water treatment plants.
- KITE is a portable, modular industrial-control training platform using real components.
- Robotics and Autonomous Systems activities cover AI/ML, computer vision, and autonomous robotics.
- CyberCity is a smart-city cyber-physical testbed spanning agriculture, transportation, energy, water, and IoT-enabled buildings.
- HoneyNet is a controlled honeypot environment used to study real-world attack behavior and threat intelligence.

Source: https://wvstateu.edu/college-of-business-sciences-and-technology/cic/

### Research and selected grants
CIC research connects cybersecurity, AI, computer science, IoT, cloud computing, OT/ICS, and critical infrastructure. Topics highlighted by the center include IoT attack detection, DDoS defense, SQL injection detection, SCADA/ICS security, phishing detection, and intrusion detection. The WVSU faculty profile lists selected awards including a $1,000,000 Google Cybersecurity Clinics Fund grant, a $749,973 U.S. Department of Energy and Marshall University critical-infrastructure project, a $500,000 CIC expansion partnership, and a $764,792 Kanawha County Commission grant supporting the CIC. These are selected page-listed awards, not a complete or independently audited funding total. Publication totals vary between page snapshots; check the current source for updated lists and counts.
